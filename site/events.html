The offer

A video podcast studio, on your show floor.

Your event gathers the people the whole industry wants to hear from. Most of what they say is heard once, by the room, and then it is gone. We record it properly and give it a second life.

The studio comes to you

We bring the set, cameras, lighting and microphones, and build a working video podcast studio inside your venue.

Your speakers, in long form

A stage slot is short. We sit your headline speakers down for the full conversation, the one delegates wish they had heard.

Your event's name on every episode

Each episode is titled and credited as recorded at your event, on screen and in the description, wherever it is watched.

Published where the industry watches

Full video on YouTube. Audio on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and Amazon Music. Shared with our network on LinkedIn.

Clips that keep it travelling

After the event we cut short clips from each interview for your social channels and ours, so the conversations keep reaching people long after the show.

A studio your sponsors can own

The studio is a new piece of inventory for your sales team: naming rights, a branded backdrop and guest slots for your sponsors.

How it works

From first call to final episode.

One plan, three legs. Your team's work is mostly at the start: after that we run the studio and the releases.

Pre-flight · before the event

Plan the line-up

  • We agree the guest list with your speaker team.
  • We fix where the studio sits and the recording schedule.
  • Both of us announce the partnership and the guests.
On the show floor · during

Record on site

  • The studio is built and tested before doors open.
  • We record with your speakers, exhibitors and sponsors through the day.
  • Delegates can stop and watch conversations being made.
After landing · the months that follow

Release and report

  • Episodes come out one at a time, each crediting your event.
  • You receive clips and highlights for your own marketing.
  • A report on views and reach is available on request.

Questions organisers ask

The practical side.

What do you need from us?
A space on or near the show floor, power, and early sight of your speaker schedule. We bring everything else.
Who chooses the guests?
We build the list together. You know who is speaking and who your sponsors want to put forward. We know which conversations our audience watches.
When do the episodes come out?
After the event, one at a time, so your event keeps appearing in the industry's feeds for months instead of a single week.
What does it cost?
It depends on the format and the length of the event. Tell us about yours and we will come back with a proposal.
