About the show

The aviation industry's video podcast.

The Flying High Club is a long-form video podcast about the business of flight. Every episode sits down with someone running a part of the industry — airline CEOs, airport innovation chiefs, ground-handling leaders, loyalty heads, travel-tech founders and investors — to talk honestly about strategy, leadership, technology and what comes next.

Since 2023 the show has featured leaders from IATA, Kenya Airways, Swissport, Emirates Skywards, IAG, Microsoft, flyadeal and Miami International Airport. It is the Official Podcast Partner of the World Aviation Festival, and full episodes are on YouTube, Spotify, Apple Podcasts and Amazon Music.

Questions

Frequently asked.

What is The Flying High Club?

The Flying High Club is an aviation industry video podcast. Each episode is a long-form conversation with a leader shaping aviation — airline CEOs, airport executives, founders, technologists and investors — about strategy, leadership, technology, sustainability and careers in the industry. It launched in 2023 and is the Official Podcast Partner of the World Aviation Festival.

Who has been a guest on The Flying High Club?

Guests include Willie Walsh, Director General of IATA; George Kamal, CEO of Kenya Airways; Warwick Brady, CEO of Swissport; Nejib Ben-Khedher, SVP of Emirates Skywards; Jonathon Counsell, Group Head of Sustainability at IAG; Daniel Williams, CTO Travel at Microsoft UK; Steven Greenway, CEO of flyadeal; Irra Ariella Khi, founder of Zamna; aviation creator Sam Chui; and Miami International Airport with Air Sheriff.

Where can I watch or listen to The Flying High Club?

Full video episodes are on YouTube. Audio episodes are on Spotify, Apple Podcasts and Amazon Music, and the show is also active on LinkedIn.

Who is the podcast for?

People who work in or around aviation and travel: airline and airport professionals, travel-tech and aerospace teams, investors, agencies and partners, and anyone considering a career in the industry who wants to hear directly from its leaders.

Is The Flying High Club an airline or airport podcast?

Both, and more. Episodes cover airlines, airports, ground handling, loyalty, travel technology, sustainable aviation fuel, AI and aviation startups, so it suits anyone looking for an airline podcast, an airport podcast or a broader aviation and travel industry podcast.
